Weekly coordination meeting - notes

Attendees: three team leads and the facilitator.

1. Release calendar. The next release window opens on the first Tuesday of the
month. Changes must be merged two days before the window. Anything later moves
to the following window unless the facilitator approves an exception.

2. Shared drive clean-up. Each team lead will archive folders that have not
changed in the last year and report the number of archived folders next week.

3. Training. A one-hour session on writing clear decision records is scheduled
for the third Thursday. Attendance is optional; the recording will be shared.

Actions:
- Team leads: archive stale folders and report counts.
- Facilitator: publish the release calendar on the team page.
- Everyone: review the draft decision-record template before Thursday.
