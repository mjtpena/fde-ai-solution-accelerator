## Review and contingency

- Capacity: 9 planned days plus 1 reserve day at 800 AUD/day = 8,000 AUD.
  Role consultations are included, not additional charged person-days.
- Check-ins: daily role-owned action review; Sponsor attends entry and exit gates.
- Stop conditions: failed boundary/injection check or unavailable prerequisite.
- Delay response: escalate to Sponsor within one working day; record revised
  scope, fee and dates before restarting affected work.
- Release authority: Evaluation reviewer, Security reviewer and Operations owner
  must concur; Sponsor authorises the staged demonstration only after that.
- Rollback authority: Operations owner can stop staging immediately; Security
  reviewer must concur before restart after a safety incident.
